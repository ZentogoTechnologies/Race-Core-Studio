SOFTWARE LICENCE AGREEMENT — RACE CORE STUDIO

THIS IS A PROVISIONAL TEXT. Before selling, it must be replaced by an
agreement drafted by a lawyer. What follows describes how the software
actually behaves, so that it can serve as a brief for whoever drafts it,
but it is not a legally reviewed document.

This is a translation of the Spanish original for the convenience of
English-speaking customers. Where the two differ, the Spanish text
governs.

1. SUBJECT

Zentogo Technologies grants the Customer a non-exclusive,
non-transferable licence to use the Race Core Studio software, on the
terms set out below.

2. ONE LICENCE PER COMPUTER

The licence permits use on ONE (1) computer. On activation it becomes
permanently tied to that computer's identifier. Moving it to another
computer requires the express authorisation of Zentogo Technologies.

3. PLANS AND TERM

   Standard   Monthly. Licensing.
   Premium    Monthly. Licensing and maintenance; includes the features
              released during its term.
   Platinum   Annual. Everything above.

The monthly plans require periodic online verification. A prolonged
absence of connectivity does not interrupt service immediately: that is
what the grace period is for.

4. EXPIRY

When the licence expires, a grace period of SEVEN (7) calendar days
begins, during which the software keeps operating and warns of its
situation. Once that period is exhausted, the software blocks and stops
operating.

Blocking does NOT delete any of the Customer's data. Drivers, vehicles,
categories, events, photographs, logos, race history and settings remain
intact on the computer, and become available again on renewal.

5. CUSTOMER DATA

Data entered or generated by the Customer belongs to the Customer. It is
stored on the Customer's own computer. Zentogo Technologies does not
access it and does not transmit it outside the Customer's computer.

6. UPDATES

Security and bug-fix updates are released for every plan. New features
are enabled according to the plan contracted. No update is applied
without the Customer accepting it.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The software is the property of Zentogo Technologies. This licence
transfers no ownership. Decompilation, reverse engineering,
redistribution and assignment to third parties are prohibited.

8. THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS

Race Core Studio incorporates CasparCG (graphics server) and MongoDB
(database), each subject to its own licence, which is distributed with
it and prevails over this agreement as regards that component.
